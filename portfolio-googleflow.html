Google Flow AI Video Generation

Exploring Generative AI Video Workflows with Google Flow (Veo2 Model) for High-Impact Commercial Storytelling.

About the Project

This project explores state-of-the-art Generative AI Video Production using Google Flow powered by Google's Veo2 video generation model. The goal was to master prompt engineering and temporal flow controls to produce commercial-grade, photorealistic cinematic sequences with fluid transitions, fluid dynamics (water & liquid gold), and character consistency.

Key Features

  • Veo2 Generative Engine: Leveraged Google Flow's Veo2 model to synthesize 1080p high-framerate clips with physics-accurate fluid and metallic simulation.
  • Multi-Shot Cinematic Sequence: Combined macro water-splash closeups, liquid gold melting over watch surfaces, and seamless cuts into real-world character scenes.
  • Temporal & Spatial Prompt Control: Applied advanced text-to-video and image-to-video prompts to control camera movement, focus pulls, and lighting shifts.
  • Commercial Production Workflow: Engineered a streamlined pipeline converting AI-generated video assets into a cohesive luxury product commercial.

What I Learnt

  • AI Prompt Crafting for Motion: Mastering textual descriptions for lighting, lens focal length, fluid simulation, and micro-movements.
  • Controlling Video Consistency: Maintaining character features, product aesthetics, and color grading across disparate generated scenes.
  • Physics & Fluid Generation: Understanding how AI models interpret complex physical interactions like water ripples, gold liquid viscosity, and metallic reflection.
  • Workflow Integration: Combining Generative AI tools with traditional editing workflows to produce production-ready media.

Tech & Tool Stack

  • AI Platform: Google Flow
  • Base Model: Google Veo 2 (Text-to-Video / Image-to-Video)
  • Output Format: 1080p MP4 / High-bitrate Video
  • Domain: AI Film & Commercial Production
