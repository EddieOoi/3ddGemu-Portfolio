Role Overview & Impact

During my tenure at THEXRA SDN BHD as an Associate Solutions Engineer and XR Coordinator, I led and supported the full-lifecycle engineering of enterprise-grade Augmented and Virtual Reality (AR/VR) applications. My primary focus centered on building immersive XR and AI-driven solutions tailored for industrial compliance, safety training, and workflow digitization.

Additionally, I conducted targeted R&D for strategic corporate proposals, engineered custom 3D assets, configured multi-sensor hardware setups, and designed an end-to-end XR development curriculum for the CelcomDigi Metaversity Internship Programme, where I successfully mentored a 5-person engineering team through complete project execution.

Key Highlights & Responsibilities

  • Industrial Safety Training: Engineered immersive XR/AI applications leveraging game mechanics to simplify complex safety compliance standards.
  • Workflow Digitization: Digitized intricate operational procedures into spatial environments, significantly cutting onboarding times for new hires.
  • Leadership & Mentorship: Designed and executed an end-to-end XR curriculum for the CelcomDigi Metaversity Internship, guiding 5 interns through a structured lifecycle.
  • Deployments & Hardware Configuration: Managed spatial sensors, VR/AR headsets, and technical setups on-site for high-profile client exhibitions, live demos, and workshops.
  • 3D Asset Pipeline: Created, optimized, and integrated enterprise-ready 3D models and interactive elements into the VIRNECT platform.

Key Technical & Professional Skills Gained

  • VIRNECT SDK Integration: Mastery in utilizing the VIRNECT ecosystem for industrial productivity, remote collaboration, and digital twin visualization.
  • Enterprise XR Engineering: Designing robust AR/VR solutions optimized for performance in real-world industrial environments.
  • AI-Driven Industrial Solutions: Blending artificial intelligence with virtual simulations to create adaptive, interactive safety scenarios.
  • On-Site Hardware & Spatial Setup: Hands-on expertise in configuring spatial sensors, enterprise headsets, and resolving real-time hardware bottlenecks under pressure.
